# 📊 FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Analysis Dashboard for FNP (Ferns N Petals)** built using **Microsoft Excel**. The dashboard analyzes sales performance across multiple dimensions such as **occasions, months, product categories, products, locations, and order time**.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights using **KPIs, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Timeline Filters**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The dashboard answers key business questions:

- How many orders were placed?
- What is the total revenue generated?
- What is the average order-delivery time?
- What is the average customer spend?
- Which occasions generate higher revenue?
- How does revenue vary by month?
- Which product categories and products perform best?
- Which locations contribute the most revenue?
- At what time do customers generate higher sales?

# 📊 Dashboard Analysis

## 🎉 Revenue by Occasion

Analyzes revenue across occasions including:

- Anniversary
- Birthday
- Diwali
- Holi
- Raksha Bandhan
- Valentine's Day

This analysis helps identify important events for targeted marketing, promotional campaigns, and inventory planning.

## 📅 Revenue by Month

Tracks monthly revenue trends and helps identify:

- Peak sales periods
- Seasonal demand patterns
- Revenue fluctuations

## 🏙️ Revenue by Location

Analyzes sales performance across different locations to identify high-performing markets and regional opportunities.

## 🛍️ Revenue by Category

Compares revenue across product categories such as:

- Cake
- Colors
- Mugs
- Plants
- Raksha Bandhan
- Soft Toys
- Sweets

## 🏆 Top Products by Revenue

Identifies products generating the highest revenue and supports decisions related to inventory planning, promotions, and product recommendations.

## ⏰ Revenue by Hour

Analyzes customer purchasing behavior throughout the day to identify high-activity periods and opportunities for targeted marketing.
